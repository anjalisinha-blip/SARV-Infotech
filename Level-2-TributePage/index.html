Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam

Missile Man of India | Scientist | Former President

Early life and Education

  • Birth :- Born on October 15,1931, in Rameshwaram , Tamil Nadu , to Jainulabdeen and Ashiamma.

  • Struggle :- Grew up in humble circumstances and sold newspapers as a boy to help support his family.

  • Studies :- Graduated with a degree in physics from St. Joseph's College , Tiruchirappalli, in 1954 and specialized in aeronautical engineering from the Madras Institute of Technology(MIT) in 1960.

Why I Admire Him

Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam is one of the most inspiring personalities in India. He believed that dreams transform into thoughts and thoughts result in action. His dedication, simplicity, and love for education motivate millions of students. I admire his love for education, his honesty, and his service to the nation. As a scientist and the former President of India, he devoted his life to the country's development. His words and actions continue to motivate millions of young people to believe in themselves and achieve their goals.

Scientific and Space Career

  • DRDO and ISRO :- Joined the Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO) in 1960 and later moved to the Indian Space Research Organisation (ISRO) in 1969.

  • SLV-III :-Served as project director for India's first indigenous Satellite Launch Vehicle, which successfully deployed the Rohini satellite in 1980.

  • Missile Program :- Led the development of key guided missiles like Agni and Prithvi, earning him the title "Missile Man of India".

  • Pokhran-II :- Played a vital technical and organizational role in India's 1998 nuclear tests.

Major Books and Literary Legacy

  • Wings of Fire(1999) :- Wings of Fire is the 1999 autobiography of Indian aerospace scientist and former President of India, A. P. J. Abdul Kalam. It was written by Kalam and Arun Tiwari.

  • Ignited Minds :- Ignited Minds: Unleashing the Power Within India is a book written by Dr. A. P. J. Abdul Kalam, President of India from 2002 to 2007. Dr. Kalam dedicated Ignited Minds to an intermediate school child he met at a school. While talking to students, a question came up: "Who is our enemy?"

Major National Awards

  • Bharat Ratna (1997): India's highest civilian award, given for his stellar work in modernizing India's defense technology and scientific research.
  • Padma Vibhushan (1990): India's second-highest civilian award, recognizing his exceptional service to the nation.
  • Padma Bhushan (1981): India's third-highest civilian award, honoring his early pioneering work in aerospace and rocketry.
  • Indira Gandhi Award for National Integration (1997): Presented by the Indian National Congress for promoting national unity and understanding.
  • Veer Savarkar Award (1998): Awarded for his contributions to national defense and indigenous missile development.

International and Special Awards

  • SASTRA Ramanujan Prize (2000): Awarded by SASTRA University for his affinity and contributions to mathematics and science education.
  • King Charles II Medal (2007): Presented by the Royal Society (UK) for his vision of promoting science and technology.
  • International von Kármán Wings Award (2009): Conferred by the International Council of the Aeronautical Sciences for aerospace science achievements.
  • Hoover Medal (2009): Awarded by the ASME Foundation and engineering societies in the U.S. for public service and humanitarian efforts.
  • Von Braun Award (2013): Given by the National Space Society for excellence in space management and leadership.
  • IEEE Honorary Membership (2011): Recognized by the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers for global technological leadership.

Presidency and Later Years

  • President of India :- Served from 2002 to 2007, earning widespread endearment as the "People's President" due to his humble nature and passion for connecting with youth.

  • Author and Educator :- Wrote influential books like Wings of Fire and Ignited Minds, and spent his final years teaching and lecturing.

  • Death :- Passed away on July 27, 2015, after collapsing during a lecture at the Indian Institute of Management Shillong.
